A plain rendered <table> — the kind of thing a "corporate app" shows and an LLM would otherwise try to add up in its head (and get wrong). Point ml.pythonExec at it with { table: "#sales" } and it lands in the sandbox as a pandas df for exact math.

Try:

await ml.agent("What's the grand total of all quarters, the top rep by annual total, and the highest-grossing region? Use python_exec on the #sales table — don't eyeball it.", { extraTools: [ml.pythonTool()] })
